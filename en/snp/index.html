FAQ

FAQ

Can I apply with no live experience?

Yes. In the first four weeks of onboarding we build everything together, from setup to running a stream.

Is a small follower count OK?

We care more about whether you can stream consistently than about follower numbers. Feel free to apply.

Where do I stream from?

Anywhere you like, including home. We’ll guide your setup based on the gear you have.

What are the contract terms?

They depend on your experience and goals, so we explain them individually during the consultation.

I’m currently with another agency.

Let us know your current situation during the consultation and we’ll look at the options together.

Can creators outside Korea apply?

Yes. Add your country in the form and we’ll guide you during the consultation.
