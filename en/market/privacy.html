HOMI FACTORY Co., Ltd. (the "Company") establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy as follows, pursuant to Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act, in order to protect the personal information of data subjects and to handle related grievances promptly and smoothly. This Policy applies to the HOMI MARKET website (https://homifactory.com/market) operated by the Company.

Article 1 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information)

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal information processed will not be used for any purpose other than the following, and if the purpose of use changes, the Company will take necessary measures, such as obtaining separate consent pursuant to Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

  1. Receipt and processing of marketing consultation requests (project briefs): reviewing request details, proposing customized marketing plans, contacting the requester to conduct consultations, and notifying processing results
  2. Responding to inquiries and handling grievances: reviewing inquiries, contacting the data subject for fact-finding, and notifying processing results
  3. Website usage statistics: improving services by analyzing visits and usage (used in statistical form that cannot identify individuals)
  4. Live chat consultation: reviewing and responding to chat inquiries, and managing consultation history

Article 2 (Items of Personal Information Processed)

  1. Project brief (free consultation request) — Required: name, contact (phone number), email, brand/product name, consultation details (direction, target countries/markets, concerns, goals)
    Optional: brand link (website/social media address), current sales channels, preferred timing, budget range
  2. Information automatically generated and collected while using the website: IP address, cookies, date and time of visit, service usage records, browser and device information
  3. Live chat consultation — consultation type, consultation details, and name and contact (phone number or email) for receiving a response

※ Users are advised not to enter sensitive information such as resident registration numbers or health information in the consultation details field, and the Company does not request such information.

Article 3 (Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information)

① The Company processes and retains personal information within the retention and use period prescribed by law or the retention and use period consented to by the data subject at the time of collection.

② The processing and retention periods for each category of personal information are as follows.

  1. Project brief (consultation request) information: 1 year from the date the consultation is completed. However, if the consultation leads to a contract, the information is retained for the period prescribed by relevant laws (the Commercial Act, the Framework Act on National Taxes, etc.) for contract performance and resolution of related disputes.
  2. Website access records: 3 months (the Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
  3. Live chat consultation records: 1 year from the date the consultation ends

③ Notwithstanding the above periods, if a related investigation or inquiry is in progress, the information is retained until such investigation or inquiry is concluded.

Article 4 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

① The Company processes the personal information of data subjects only within the scope of the purposes stated in Article 1, and provides personal information to third parties only in cases falling under Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act, such as with the consent of the data subject or under special provisions of law.

② The Company currently does not provide personal information to third parties. If it becomes necessary to provide personal information to overseas local partners or others in the course of consultations or campaigns, the Company will notify the data subject in advance of the recipient, purpose of provision, items provided, and retention and use period, and obtain separate consent.

Article 7 (Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information)

① The Company destroys personal information without delay when it is no longer necessary, such as when the retention period has expired or the purpose of processing has been achieved.

② If personal information must continue to be preserved under other laws, the Company preserves it by transferring it to a separate database (DB) or storing it in a different location.

③ Information in electronic file form is deleted using technical methods that prevent recovery or reproduction, and paper documents are destroyed by shredding or incineration.

Article 8 (Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives and How to Exercise Them)

① Data subjects may at any time exercise their rights against the Company, including requests to access, correct, delete, or suspend processing of personal information and to withdraw consent.

② Rights may be exercised in writing, by email, or by other means, and the Company will take action without delay (within 10 days from the date the request is received).

③ Rights may also be exercised through a representative, such as the data subject’s legal representative or a person delegated by the data subject. In this case, a power of attorney in accordance with Form No. 11 annexed to the Notice on Personal Information Processing Methods must be submitted.

④ The rights of data subjects to request access to and suspension of processing of personal information may be restricted pursuant to Article 35(4) and Article 37(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act.

⑤ Deletion of personal information may not be requested where other laws specify that such personal information is subject to collection.

⑥ The Company verifies whether the person exercising the rights is the data subject or a legitimate representative.

Article 9 (Personal Information of Children Under 14)

The Company’s website and consultation services are intended for business and brand representatives, and the Company does not collect personal information of children under the age of 14.

Article 10 (Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information)

Pursuant to Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Company takes the following measures necessary to ensure security.

  1. Managerial measures: establishment and implementation of an internal management plan, minimization of staff handling personal information, and regular training
  2. Technical measures: management of access rights to and access control of the personal information processing system, retention of access records and prevention of their forgery or alteration, encryption of data in transit (HTTPS), and installation and updating of security programs
  3. Physical measures: access control to locations where materials containing personal information are stored

Article 11 (Installation, Operation, and Refusal of Devices that Automatically Collect Personal Information)

① The Company uses cookies, which store usage information and retrieve it from time to time, to provide users with suitable information.

② Purpose of cookie use: statistical analysis of visits and usage (Google Analytics). Conversations in the live chat consultation window are temporarily stored in the browser’s session storage and are deleted when the browser is closed.

③ Installation, operation, and refusal of cookies: you may refuse cookie storage through your web browser settings (e.g. Chrome — Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies; Safari — Settings > Privacy).

④ Refusing cookie storage does not affect your use of the website or your ability to request a consultation.
