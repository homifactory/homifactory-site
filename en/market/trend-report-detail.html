Mega, macro, micro, nano: results by tier in numbers, and how to use each.

HOMI MARKET Report Team · 2026.10 · 7 min read
Trend Report 2026
TREND REPORT 01
01

TL;DR in 3 lines

  • The more followers, the lower the engagement — TikTok nano 10.3%, micro 8.7%, mid-tier 7.5%
  • Mega influencers’ sway in Southeast Asia and Latin America fell within a year, while smaller creators keep consumers’ trust
  • Still, the answer isn’t “go small” — it’s the mix. Each tier plays a different role

What changed in 2026

This year, brands are shifting budget from a single mega influencer to smaller creators. There are two reasons. The first is engagement. In influencer marketing tracking research, average TikTok engagement was 10.3% for nano (under 10,000 followers), 8.7% for micro, and 7.5% for mid-tier — the smaller the creator, the higher the rate. Instagram shows the same steep curve, from 6.23% for nano down to 0.92% for mega.

The second is trust. In a survey of about 2,400 consumers across 6 Southeast Asian countries by impact.com and Cube, 59% said they are influenced by mega influencers (1M+ followers) — lower than a year earlier. Smaller creators, meanwhile, were reported to have lost far less trust.

So should we only use nano creators?

No. Engagement shows “how deep,” while follower count shows “how wide.” The reactions created by 100 nano creators and the buzz created by one mega creator can’t replace each other. With 87.7% of TikTok creators and 75.9% of Instagram creators at nano scale, small creators are plentiful — which is exactly why selection criteria and management decide performance.

The role of each tier

Mega · Celebrities are “the face of the brand.” One person’s name and image become a symbol of the brand — used to make it memorable in a first global launch or a rebrand. Measure success by “brand search volume” and “mentions” rather than impressions.

Macro means “broad reach.” Through web dramas and polished content, it reaches many people in a short time. It fits new product launches and seasonal campaigns; also track views and secondary content use.

Micro means “multiplication.” When many creators with clear niches post at the same time, your brand becomes “what everyone’s using lately.” One analysis finds that 50~70% of everyday content on TikTok Indonesia comes from micro-tier creators. Measure by saves and link clicks to your store.

Nano · Product Testers mean “real-use reviews.” The more reviews there are, the less hesitation right before purchase. Measure by review count, search visibility, and purchase conversion.

A mix, not a single creator — in the right order

One K-brand campaign in Los Angeles opened with a mega influencer on the front page, then rolled out 600 local micro creators in sequence, generating 30,000,000 cumulative impressions in 3 days. HOMI MARKET designs campaigns on this principle: set the order in which tiers are used, and measure each tier’s results with its own metrics.
