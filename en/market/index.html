FAQ

FAQ

Do consultations and plan proposals cost anything?
Consultations are free. We listen to your brand’s goals and situation and help you figure out which country and approach fit. Nothing is charged until you decide to proceed.
Can we work with you on a smaller budget?
Yes. We design from the smallest viable scope based on the nature and size of your campaign. Feel free to reach out.
Can we choose the influencers ourselves?
We share our recommendations first and fine-tune them together. If you have preferred creators, we review why and reflect it in the plan.
Which countries do you operate in?
We operate through our Japan and Indonesia branches and partners in China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines, and North America.
Can you connect us to local online retail listings or live commerce?
Yes. We have partnerships that cover commerce listings and live commerce operations.
How do you handle ad disclosures?
We operate transparently in line with each country’s ad disclosure rules (KFTC, FTC, etc.).
Can we use the content in our own ads or product pages?
Yes. We agree on the scope of secondary use in advance and secure copyright and likeness usage rights together.
How do we check results?
We share them through a live dashboard plus interim and final reports.
What do we need to prepare?
Just tell us your brand, product, current challenge, and goal. We’ll sort out the rest together.
